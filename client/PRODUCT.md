# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Users
Residents are the primary audience. Visitors and potential investors are also served.

## Product Purpose
The Sidama Regional State website helps people find public services, regional information, news, and ways to contact the administration.

## Positioning
No distinctive product mechanism beyond the regional public-information role has been confirmed.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Preserve the existing routes, features, and English, Amharic, and Sidaamu Afoo language support.
- Keep sample news and service listings clearly identified as illustrative, not official information.
- The site is a Next.js application.

## Brand Commitments
- Preserve the Sidama Regional State name and existing mark.
- Preserve the existing three-language experience.

## Evidence on Hand
- Existing website copy, routes, and assets are in the client application.
- Current starter news and service listings are explicitly described as illustrative sample content.

## Product Principles
- Make public information and services easy for residents to find.
- Keep regional information understandable to visitors and investors.
- Distinguish sample content from verified official information.
- Preserve access across the site's supported languages.
